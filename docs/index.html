Court402

When AI agents pay each other with x402, payment is instant — disputes are not. Court402 escrows the payment in a GenLayer Intelligent Contract and lets the Internet Court rule on delivery: the contract fetches the evidence, an AI jury judges it against the agreed terms, and the escrow settles itself.

How it works

  1. 402 handshake — the seller answers 402 Payment Required, pointing at the escrow contract instead of a direct payment.
  2. Escrow — the buyer locks GEN with natural-language delivery terms + an evidence URL.
  3. Delivery — the seller delivers and marks it on-chain.
  4. Confirm or dispute — the buyer confirms (escrow released) or files a dispute.
  5. The Court rules — validators fetch the evidence, an LLM jury judges terms vs. delivery, and the contract pays out or refunds automatically.
